HELLO

I'm Savannah.

Art has always been a way to slow down, pay attention, and turn an idea into something real.

Savannah's Art Works was created around that simple idea: make beautiful things by hand and share them with people who appreciate the time and care that goes into original artwork.

Every piece is created individually. That means no two paintings are exactly alike, and every finished piece carries a little bit of the process that created it.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Art doesn't have to be complicated to matter.

A painting can change the feeling of a room. It can remind you of a place, a person, a season, or a moment you never want to forget.

That's what I want these pieces to do. I want them to become part of someone's home and eventually part of their story.
